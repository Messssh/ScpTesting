# SCP Facility Management - Deep Simulation Overhaul\n\n## 🎮 Overview\n\nThis comprehensive upgrade transforms the SCP Facility Management game into a deep simulation experience with 10 major systems, while maintaining **100% backward compatibility** with existing saves and gameplay.\n\n## 📦 What's New\n\n### 1. **Staff AI Overhaul** (npc-system.js)\n- NPCs now have **names, roles, and personalities**\n- Track individual needs: **energy, hunger, stress, morale, health**\n- **Dynamic job assignment** based on priorities\n- Productivity affected by working conditions\n- Staff morale impacts research speed and error rates\n\n**Features:**\n- Scientists work from offices, perform research, take breaks\n- Security guards patrol and detect breaches\n- Engineers repair damaged rooms and generators\n- Doctors heal injured staff\n- Class-D stay in holding cells, participate in tests\n- MTF operatives deploy during emergencies\n\n### 2. **Door & Lockdown System** (door-lockdown-system.js)\n- **Functional doors** with clearance levels\n- Standard, Security, Heavy, and Blast door types\n- Power requirements for each door type\n- **Facility lockdown** with progressive levels (Yellow/Red/Full)\n- Automatic lockdown during breaches\n- Door animations and state management\n\n**Features:**\n- Staff can only pass through doors matching their clearance\n- Heavy doors seal during emergencies\n- Security checkpoints distribute staff efficiently\n- Lockdown system cuts power to non-critical zones\n\n### 3. **Unique SCP Behaviours** (scp-behaviors.js)\nEach SCP has unique, interactive mechanics:\n\n- **SCP-173 (The Sculpture)**: Moves when unobserved, kills nearby staff\n- **SCP-096 (Shy Guy)**: Enters rage mode if viewed, hunts observer until dead\n- **SCP-049 (Plague Doctor)**: Hunts Class-D, can infect them with \"cure\"\n- **SCP-999 (Tickle Monster)**: Increases morale of nearby staff\n- **SCP-682 (Hard-to-Destroy Reptile)**: Slowly damages containment, adapts\n- **SCP-131 (Eye Pods)**: Safe and docile, curious behavior\n\n**Extensible:** Register new SCP behaviors with custom logic.\n\n### 4. **Dynamic Events System** (events-system.js)\nRandom facility emergencies with real consequences:\n\n- **Power Grid Failure**: All power cuts, backup generators kick in\n- **Facility Fire**: Random room damage, staff evacuation\n- **Class-D Riot**: Holding cell breach, staff hostage situations\n- **Generator Explosion**: Destroy power infrastructure\n- **Seismic Event**: Structural damage to rooms\n- **Gas Leak**: Staff take damage, stress increases\n- **Multiple Containment Breach**: Several SCPs breach at once\n\nEach event has severity levels (low/moderate/high/critical) with different durations and consequences.\n\n### 5. **Research Tree** (research-tree.js)\n20+ researches across 6 categories:\n\n- **Containment**: Better cell design, reinforcement, acoustic isolation\n- **Security**: Advanced surveillance, elite MTF training, automated lockdown\n- **Personnel**: Enhanced researcher training, staff accommodations, rapid response\n- **Engineering**: Preventative maintenance, automated repairs, reinforced walls\n- **Medical**: Field medicine, trauma surgery, stress management\n- **Power**: High-capacity batteries, nuclear generators, emergency protocols\n\n**Features:**\n- Prerequisites must be completed first\n- Each research unlocks gameplay improvements\n- Progress tracking and time-based completion\n\n### 6. **Expanded Power System** (power-system.js)\n- **Power consumption per room type**\n- **Multiple power sources**: Generators, backup generators, batteries\n- **Priority-based power allocation**: Critical rooms stay powered first\n- **Room degradation**: Unpowered rooms deteriorate 10x faster\n- **Electrical efficiency**: Distribution networks boost supply\n- **Battery storage**: Buffer against power spikes\n- **Emergency shutdowns**: Non-critical systems offline during crises\n\n### 7. **NPC Needs System**\nEach NPC has four core needs:\n\n- **Hunger**: Increases over time, requires break room visits\n- **Energy**: Depletes with work, restored in break rooms\n- **Stress**: Increases from dangerous situations, reduced by morale\n- **Health**: Damaged by injuries, restored by medical bays\n\n**Impact:**\n- Low energy = reduced productivity\n- High hunger = staff distraction\n- High stress = mistakes and morale loss\n- Poor health = inability to work\n\n### 8. **Incident Reports** (incident-reporting.js)\nAuto-generated reports after major events:\n\n- SCP involved\n- Casualties count\n- Property damage\n- Time to recontain\n- Financial cost\n- Root cause\n- Severity classification\n- Historical tracking\n\n### 9. **Facility Statistics** (facility-statistics.js)\nComprehensive dashboard with:\n\n- Total staff and SCPs\n- Casualties and breaches\n- Research completed\n- Facility efficiency rating (0-100%)\n- Containment rating (0-100%)\n- Power usage and uptime\n- Average staff morale\n- Risk assessment level (LOW/MODERATE/HIGH/CRITICAL)\n\n### 10. **Code Quality** (All modular files)\n✅ **Separation of Concerns**\n- NPC Logic → npc-system.js\n- SCP Logic → scp-behaviors.js\n- Research → research-tree.js\n- Economy → (Main game loop)\n- Power → power-system.js\n- Building → (Main game loop)\n- Events → events-system.js\n- UI → ui-enhancements.js\n\n✅ **Performance Optimizations**\n- Memoized pathfinding\n- Throttled event checks\n- Efficient grid iteration\n- Lazy rendering\n\n✅ **Extensibility**\n- Register new SCPs easily\n- Add new research nodes\n- Create custom events\n- Extend NPC behaviors\n\n## 🚀 Integration Instructions\n\n### Quick Start\n\n1. **Copy all module files** from `modules/`
